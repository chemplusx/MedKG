Translational informatics Molecular Diversity (2025)

Advancing biomedical research with graph-native infrastructure

MedKG is an applied research project led by the Department of Pharmacoinformatics at the National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER), S.A.S. Nagar. The initiative builds open tooling that lowers the barrier to constructing biomedical knowledge graphs, running graph machine learning, and delivering trustworthy analytics through modern APIs. The peer-reviewed study in Molecular Diversity details MedKG's 35-source integration, CI/CU pipeline, and MedLINK benchmarks.

Community stewardship

The repository is open for collaboration. Contributions spanning new ontologies, improved Cypher queries, performance tuning, or analytical notebooks are welcome via pull requests. Please reference relevant datasets and include reproducible scripts with each submission.

Open research

The project emphasises transparent pipelines, open metrics, and scripts that can be reproduced on academic or industrial infrastructure.

Collaborative ecosystem

MedKG is designed for integration with clinical partners, academic labs, and AI research groups exploring multimodal reasoning.

Responsible usage

Respect data licences, maintain provenance, and communicate limitations when deploying graph-derived insights.
