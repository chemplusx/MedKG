MedKG: research infrastructure for biomedical discovery

MedKG is an applied research initiative from the Department of Pharmacoinformatics at NIPER SAS Nagar. The project unifies 35 biomedical sources, maintains a CI/CU ingestion pipeline, and exposes graph-native tooling for translational medicine teams.
